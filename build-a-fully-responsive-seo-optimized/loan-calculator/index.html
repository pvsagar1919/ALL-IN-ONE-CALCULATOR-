    Step-by-step explanation

    1. Find the scheduled monthly payment using the standard amortization formula.
    2. Add any extra monthly payment to the scheduled payment.
    3. For each month, interest is charged on the remaining balance.
    4. The rest of the payment reduces principal until the loan is paid off.

    Formula used

    Payment = P x r x (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1). Extra payments reduce principal faster.

    Example calculation

    A INR 5,00,000 loan at 10.5% for 4 years has a monthly payment near INR 12,803 before extra payments.

    Frequently asked questions

    How does extra payment help?

    Extra payment reduces outstanding principal faster, which lowers future interest.

    Can this be used for personal loans?

    Yes. It works for most fixed-rate amortizing loans.

    Are fees included?

    No. Add fees to principal if they are financed into the loan.
