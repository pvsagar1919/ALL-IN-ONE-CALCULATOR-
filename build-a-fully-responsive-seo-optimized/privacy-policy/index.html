Privacy Policy

BrightCalc is designed to be private by default. Calculations run in your browser and recent calculations are stored locally on your device.

Data we store

  • Calculator inputs are processed in your browser.
  • Recent calculations are saved in local storage only when you press Calculate.
  • Contact form content is sent through your own email app.

Analytics and cookies

This generated build does not include analytics scripts or advertising cookies. If analytics are added later, disclose them here before launch.

Control

You can clear recent calculations by clearing browser site data for this website.
