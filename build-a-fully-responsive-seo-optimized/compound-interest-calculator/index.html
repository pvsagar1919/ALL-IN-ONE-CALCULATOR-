    Step-by-step explanation

    1. Convert the annual rate to a decimal and divide by compounding frequency.
    2. Raise the growth factor to the number of compounding periods.
    3. Apply the factor to principal.
    4. If monthly contributions are provided, compound each contribution using the equivalent monthly rate.

    Formula used

    A = P x (1 + r/n)^(n x t). Monthly contributions compound using the equivalent monthly rate.

    Example calculation

    INR 1,00,000 at 8% compounded monthly for 10 years grows to about INR 2,21,964 before extra contributions.

    Frequently asked questions

    What does compounding frequency mean?

    It is how often interest is added to the balance.

    Are monthly contributions made at month end?

    This calculator treats monthly contributions as made at the end of each month.

    Can I use this for fixed deposits?

    Yes, if the rate and compounding frequency match the deposit terms.
