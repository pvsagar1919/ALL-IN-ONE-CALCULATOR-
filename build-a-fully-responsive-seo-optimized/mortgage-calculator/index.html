    Step-by-step explanation

    1. Subtract down payment from home price to get the mortgage principal.
    2. Calculate monthly principal and interest using the amortization formula.
    3. Divide annual property tax and insurance by 12.
    4. Add those monthly costs to estimate the total housing payment.

    Formula used

    Monthly P&I = P x r x (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1). Total payment adds monthly taxes and insurance.

    Example calculation

    A INR 40,00,000 mortgage at 8% for 20 years has principal and interest near INR 33,458 per month.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this the same as a home loan EMI?

    The principal and interest portion uses the same EMI formula.

    Does it include maintenance charges?

    No. Add maintenance separately to your monthly budget.

    What if my down payment is larger than home price?

    The calculator validates that down payment cannot exceed home price.
