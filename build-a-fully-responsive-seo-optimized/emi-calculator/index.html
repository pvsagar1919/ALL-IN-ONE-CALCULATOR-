    Step-by-step explanation

    1. Convert the annual interest rate into a monthly decimal rate.
    2. Convert tenure into total monthly installments.
    3. Apply the EMI formula to find the fixed monthly payment.
    4. Multiply EMI by months to get total repayment, then subtract principal for total interest.

    Formula used

    EMI = P x r x (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1), where r is monthly rate and n is total months.

    Example calculation

    For INR 10,00,000 at 8.5% for 5 years, monthly EMI is about INR 20,516 and total interest is about INR 2,30,968.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is EMI?

    EMI is the fixed monthly payment made toward a loan. It includes both principal and interest.

    Does EMI change during the loan?

    For a fixed-rate loan, EMI usually stays the same. Floating-rate loans can change when the rate changes.

    Is processing fee included?

    No. This calculator estimates principal and interest only unless fees are added to the loan amount.
