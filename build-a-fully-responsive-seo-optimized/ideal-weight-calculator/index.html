    Step-by-step explanation

    1. Convert height from centimeters to inches.
    2. Find inches above 5 feet.
    3. Use the sex-specific Devine base weight.
    4. Show a practical range around the estimate because ideal weight is not a single universal number.

    Formula used

    Men: 50 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft. Women: 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft.

    Example calculation

    A 170 cm male is about 5 ft 7 in, so the Devine estimate is about 66 kg.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is ideal weight medical advice?

    No. It is a formula-based estimate, not a diagnosis or personal medical target.

    Why show a range?

    Healthy weight depends on frame size, muscle, age, and health context.

    Can short heights be calculated?

    Yes. Heights below 5 ft use the formula base without added inches.
