Understanding BMI and Calorie Estimates

Learn what BMI and calorie calculators can tell you, and where their limits are.

BMI is a screening number

BMI compares body weight with height. It is useful for broad ranges, but it does not directly measure body fat, muscle, or health markers.

Calories are estimates

Maintenance calories are usually estimated from BMR and activity level. Real needs can vary, so track progress and adjust over time.

Use ranges

Health planning works better with ranges than single perfect numbers. Combine calculator output with medical guidance when the stakes are personal or clinical.
