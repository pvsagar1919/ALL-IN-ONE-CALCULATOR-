    Step-by-step explanation

    1. Calculate basal metabolic rate from sex, age, height, and weight.
    2. Choose an activity multiplier based on typical weekly movement.
    3. Multiply BMR by activity factor to estimate maintenance calories.
    4. Use surplus or deficit targets depending on weight goal.

    Formula used

    BMR = 10W + 6.25H - 5A + S, where S is +5 for men and -161 for women. TDEE = BMR x activity factor.

    Example calculation

    A 30-year-old man, 70 kg, 170 cm, moderate activity has maintenance near 2,550 calories per day.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this exact for everyone?

    No. It is a useful estimate. Real maintenance can vary with metabolism, activity tracking, and body composition.

    How should I use cutting calories?

    A modest deficit such as 10% to 20% below maintenance is commonly used.

    How should I use bulking calories?

    A modest surplus such as 5% to 15% above maintenance is commonly used.
