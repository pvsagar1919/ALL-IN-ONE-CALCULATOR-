    Step-by-step explanation

    1. Validate that denominators are not zero.
    2. Apply the selected fraction operation.
    3. Reduce the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor.
    4. Show the exact fraction, mixed number, and decimal value.

    Formula used

    a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd. Multiply across for multiplication; divide by multiplying by reciprocal.

    Example calculation

    1/2 + 1/3 = (3 + 2) / 6 = 5/6.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can denominators be negative?

    Yes. The calculator normalizes the sign into the numerator.

    What is a reduced fraction?

    A reduced fraction divides numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor.

    How is division handled?

    Dividing by a fraction multiplies by its reciprocal.
